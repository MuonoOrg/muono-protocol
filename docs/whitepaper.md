# Muono Whitepaper

Status: working draft. Content is provisional until a dated version ships.

## Vision and mission

Vision: a token whose public record is simple to verify and difficult to counterfeit.

Mission: release only confirmed facts, show plainly which ones are pending, and keep that gap visible at a glance. Muono lives on Solana, and clarity is treated as a feature rather than decoration.

Principles:

- **Confirmed items only.** An item is announced once it is settled, never sooner.
- **One origin per fact.** A page, a post and a repository then cannot quietly disagree.
- **Plain risk.** Danger is explained in ordinary language, beside the thing it touches.

## Roadmap

The stages describe sequence, not calendar dates. Dates are added once the effort starts.

### Phase 1: Groundwork

Publish the reference site, settle the public records and prepare the repository and channels.

### Phase 2: Launch

Announce the contract address, publish the tokenomics and greet the earliest official groups.

### Phase 3: Growth

Broaden what surrounds the token, document integrations and respond to requests.

### Phase 4: Stewardship

Shift toward documented, public decision making with regular written updates.
